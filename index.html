Stream Business Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

In short: Stream Business Tycoon does not collect, share or sell any personal data. The game works entirely offline and your save stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Stream Business Tycoon does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal or usage information. Specifically, the game:

  • has no accounts or sign-in;
  • does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere;
  • contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs;
  • has no in-app purchases;
  • does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other files.

Your game save

Your progress is saved in the app's private storage on your device. Only the game can read it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include this save in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we never receive or see it. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

In-game money, ads and investing

The money, sponsors, ads, stocks and investments in Stream Business Tycoon are fictional parts of the simulation. They involve no real money and no real advertising.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
